# Tukar deck, read this per slide (English)

Word for word, one block per slide. Format is 3 minutes total: the talking slides (1 to 7
and 9) run about 90 seconds, slide 8 is the ~90 second demo, then 2 minutes of Q&A. Read it
like a person, warm and a little bit sales. Land the first line at the judges, not the
screen. Push energy on slides 3 and 4. Land the ask clearly on slide 7.

---

**SLIDE 1 — Hook**
"Every month, millions of people working abroad send money home to their families, right
from their phone. And they still pay about six percent to do it. Tukar makes that private,
on Stellar. Dollars in, local cash out, and the crossing in the middle stays private."

**SLIDE 2 — Problem to solution**
"The problem is that Stellar is a public ledger, so every payment shows the amount and both
people. That is a private financial history out in the open. So we split it. The deposit is
public, with a compliance proof. The crossing is private, the amount and both parties
hidden. The off-ramp is public again, into local cash. Public at the edges, private exactly
where a remittance shouldn't leak."

**SLIDE 3 — The moat**
"Why us. A mixer is private but can't answer a regulator. A payment link just moves crypto
between users. A normal wallet is cross-border but public. We are the only one that is
private for the user and provable to a regulator, on a real corridor. Private, compliant,
and cross-border, all three at once."

**SLIDE 4 — The market**
"And the market is huge. About six hundred sixty-nine billion dollars went into lower-income
countries in 2023, and sending two hundred dollars still costs over six percent. We make
money on a thin fee on settlement volume, paid by the anchors that route through us. One
corridor and one anchor first, then more."

**SLIDE 5 — The tech**
"None of this is hand-waving. Seven zero-knowledge circuits do the real work, and eight
contracts run live on Stellar testnet. Every proof is built in the browser and verified
on-chain."

**SLIDE 6 — Real and verified**
"It's real. Real testnet money and custody, a real trusted setup, and the off-ramp rate is
read on-chain from an oracle, so funds never move on a bad price. And it's checked, not
claimed. A genuine proof verifies, and a tampered one gets rejected."

**SLIDE 7 — Depth of integration, and the ask**
"Everything here does real work on-chain, it's load-bearing, not name-checked. The proofs
verify on-chain, the contract holds real USDC and runs the full lifecycle, compliance is
proven in-circuit, and the oracle gates settlement. What we're looking for is one
licensed-anchor pilot and a Stellar Community Fund award, to take this from testnet to a live
corridor."

**SLIDE 8 — Live demo (~90 seconds)**
"Let me show you."
[Play the 90 second video, or run it live in the four apps. If you narrate it yourself, use
the SHORT CUT lines in docs/DEMO_VO_SUBTITLES.md. Key beats: the proof building on the phone,
the on-chain off-ramp quote, a disclosure verified on-chain, and a tampered claim rejected.]

**SLIDE 9 — Close**
"That's Tukar. Real-world money, private in the middle, kept compliant at the edges, on
Stellar. It's live on testnet, not audited yet, and the fiat edges are simulated, but
everything in the middle is real and you can verify it on-chain right now. Try it, no install.
Thank you."

## If you are running long
Trim slide 4 to the first two sentences and slide 6 to one sentence. Never cut the demo or
the moat. The two lines that must survive: "Private in the middle, accountable at the
edges," and the tampered claim getting rejected on-chain.
