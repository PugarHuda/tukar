How the corridor works

01

Shielded deposit

USDC enters the pool as a commitment. A zero-knowledge compliance proof shows the source is allow-listed — without revealing who.

02

Private transfer

Value moves inside the corridor via a JoinSplit. Amounts and the sender↔receiver link stay hidden on-chain; nullifiers prevent double-spends.

03

Selective disclosure

On audit, the holder proves one fact — the amount — to a regulator. Verified on-chain by a Stellar contract. Nothing else is revealed.

04

Compliant off-ramp

The receiver withdraws and converts to local fiat at the edge, where the amount becomes visible — privacy in the middle, accountability at the edges.
